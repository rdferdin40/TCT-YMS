# TCT YMS Pricing Model

## Overview

This document outlines the pricing strategy for TCT Yard Management System, designed for the Laredo logistics market targeting cross-docks, 3PLs, freight forwarders, and warehouses handling Mexico-US freight.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

### Key Principles

1. **Value-Based Pricing**: Price reflects the value delivered (reduced detention, increased efficiency) not just cost
2. **Simplicity**: Easy to understand, no hidden fees or complex calculations
3. **Scalability**: Customers can grow without penalizing price jumps
4. **Accessibility**: Entry-level tier makes it accessible to smaller operations
5. **Predictability**: Monthly flat rate for budgeting clarity

### Market Positioning

- **Below enterprise solutions** ($5,000-15,000/month): We're not Oracle, SAP, or Manhattan Associates
- **Above DIY spreadsheets** (free but costly in hidden ways): We're professional software
- **Comparable to mid-market alternatives** ($500-2,000/month): Competitive with similar solutions

## Pricing FAQs

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, 14-day free trial with full Professional features. No credit card required.

**Q: Can I switch plans?**
A: Upgrade anytime (prorated). Downgrade at next billing cycle.

**Q: What happens if I exceed limits?**
A: We'll notify you and discuss upgrading. No automatic charges.

**Q: Are there setup fees?**
A: No. Basic setup and training included in all plans.

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: Monthly plans have no contract. Annual plans require full term commitment.

**Q: What's included in support?**
A: Email support for all tiers. Phone support for Enterprise. Priority response for Professional+.

**Q: Do prices increase?**
A: Prices are locked for 12 months from signup. We provide 90-day notice for any increases.

## Sales Guidance

### Objection Handling

**"Too expensive"**
- Calculate their detention/efficiency losses
- Show ROI payback in <3 months
- Offer Starter tier to start
- Mention 30-day guarantee

**"We're too small"**
- Starter tier designed for small operations
- Growth happens—be ready
- Competitive advantage over peers
- Small = fast implementation

**"We're too complex"**
- Enterprise tier handles complexity
- Custom integrations available

- Dedicated support included

**"We'll build our own"**
- Development cost: $50,000-200,000
